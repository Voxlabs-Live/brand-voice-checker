# Brand Voice Doc Template

Fill one of these out per client. Save it in your client folder. Paste it into the Brand Voice Checker whenever you're checking copy for that client.

**Tip:** the more concrete you make each section, the sharper the tool's flags will be. "Never use 'amazing'" beats "avoid hype language" every time.

---

## 1. Three tone words

Pick three that describe the voice. They should pull against each other a little — that's what makes a voice distinctive.

- Tone 1: ____
- Tone 2: ____
- Tone 3: ____

Example combos that work: *warm-precise*, *calm-grounded-honest*, *playful-but-rigorous*, *authoritative-warm*.

---

## 2. Banned words and phrases

Specific words/phrases the voice never uses. List the reason next to each.

- `amazing` — overused, hollow
- `literally` — filler intensifier
- `game-changer` — startup cliché
- ____
- ____
